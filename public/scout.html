Recovery checklist

Stay quiet and still. Mark exactly where it was standing and which way it ran.

Look at the arrow and the blood. The color and where they are tell you a lot about the hit.

Give it time before you track: about 30 minutes for a good lung or heart hit, several hours for a gut hit or if you are unsure. Back out quietly if in doubt.

Mark each blood spot with the button above, so you can follow the line and never lose the last one.

If you lose the trail, call for help. Many states have volunteer trackers with dogs. Check your state's recovery rules.
