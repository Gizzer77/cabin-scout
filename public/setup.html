CABINSCOUT

Quick setup. Everything stays on your computer.

Welcome

CabinScout is a free, self-hosted whitetail scouting board. It maps your land, tracks sightings and trail cams, predicts wind and thermals around your stands, and keeps a scouting log.

  • No account and no cloud. Your data lives in the data/ folder.
  • It works with zero API keys. Keys only unlock extras, and you can add them later.
  • This takes about two minutes.
