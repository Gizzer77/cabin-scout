CABINSCOUT

Quick setup. Everything stays on your computer.

Welcome

CabinScout is a free, self-hosted whitetail scouting board. It maps your land, tracks sightings and trail cams, predicts wind and thermals around your stands, and keeps a scouting log.

  • No account and no cloud. Your data lives in the data/ folder.
  • It works with zero API keys. Keys only unlock extras, and you can add them later.
  • This takes about two minutes.

Free power-ups

The app already works without any of these. Each one takes about a minute and is free. Keys stay in the .env file on this computer. Skip any you don't want.
1. Property lines (parcel map) FREE TRIAL Click any spot on the map and get the real lot lines, owner and acreage. Massachusetts works with no key. Everywhere else needs a Regrid token, or you can draw your boundary by hand (also free).
  1. Open Regrid API and create a free account.
  2. Start the free trial and copy your API token.
  3. Paste it below.
2. AI hunting advisor FREE Ask "where should I sit Saturday?" and let it read your trail-cam and scouting photos. Uses Google Gemini's free tier.
  1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with any Google account.
  2. Click Create API key, then copy it.
  3. Paste it below.
3. Phone Scout Log FREE Send photos, locations and notes from the woods with a Telegram bot of your own.
  1. Install Telegram, then open @BotFather.
  2. Send /newbot, pick a name, and copy the token it gives you.
  3. Paste it below. After setup, run npm run mobile and message your bot once.
